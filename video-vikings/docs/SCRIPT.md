# VIKINGS NEVER WORE HORNED HELMETS
### Under 7 Minutes — Short — shooting script v1

20 shots x 2.5s = 0:50, vertical 9:16, hard cuts, loops. Narration as on-screen captions (no voiceover).
Refrain: "Horns: zero."

## COLD OPEN
**A01_1** — A roaring Viking with a horned helmet on a beach beside a longship.
> Vikings. Raiders. Horned helmets.
**A01_2** — The horns pop off his helmet; he panics.
> Except... no.
**A01_3** — Title card over the beach.
> Vikings never wore horned helmets.

## SCENE 2 — THE EVIDENCE
**A02_1** — A farmer digging in a Norwegian field lifts out an old helmet.
> In 1943, a Norwegian farmer dug up a Viking helmet.
**A02_2** — Close-up of a plain rounded iron helmet with an eye guard; no people, inanimate object.
> The only near-complete one ever found.
**A02_3** — A big zero beside the helmet.
> Horns: zero.
**A02_4** — Two Vikings fight; one grabs the other's horn.
> Horns in a fight? Great way to get grabbed.

## SCENE 3 — THE REAL HORNS
**A03_1** — A Viking scratches his head, puzzled.
> So where did the horns come from?
**A03_2** — Two Bronze Age figures in horned helmets at a stone ritual site.
> Horned helmets did exist. In Bronze Age Denmark.
**A03_3** — A timeline stretches from the Bronze Age figures to a Viking far away.
> About 1,700 years before the Vikings.
**A03_4** — Question marks over the Bronze Age figures.
> Probably ceremonial. Historians still argue.

## SCENE 4 — THE MAKEOVER
**A04_1** — A 1800s painter at an easel paints a horned Viking.
> Then 1800s artists painted Vikings with horns.
**A04_2** — The painter admires his work, smug.
> Because it looked cool.
**A04_3** — An 1870s opera stage with singers in horned helmets.
> In 1876, Wagner's opera costumes went all in.
**A04_4** — A crowd in an opera house applauds.
> Audiences loved it.
**A04_5** — A stamp slams down.
> The horns stuck.

## SCENE 5 — TODAY
**A05_1** — A modern party-goer in a plastic horned helmet.
> Now they're on every costume and cartoon.
**A05_2** — A real Viking in a round helmet, deadpan.
> Real Vikings? Round helmets. No horns.
**A05_3** — The zero again.
> Horns: still zero.
**A05_4** — Back to the opening shot.
> Anyway, here's the wrong version again.

## Deliberate omissions and why
- **The Oseberg tapestry's horned figure** — almost certainly a ritual or mythological scene, not battle gear; too nuanced for 50 seconds.
- **"Vikings drank from skulls"** — a separate myth from a mistranslation; saved for its own Short.
- **Exact dates of the Veksø helmets** — given as about 900 BCE; we say "about 1,700 years before" the Viking Age (793 CE onward).

## Sources (for the description)
- Gjermundbu helmet, found 1943 at Gjermundbu farm, Norway (Museum of Cultural History, Oslo).
- Veksø helmets, Bronze Age, about 900 BCE (National Museum of Denmark).
- Carl Emil Doepler's costume designs for Wagner's Der Ring des Nibelungen, Bayreuth, 1876.
