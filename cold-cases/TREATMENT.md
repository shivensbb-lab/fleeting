# History Cold Cases — series treatment (Halftone Dossier)

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 28–33 s each, narrated (Kokoro am_onyx, dry report voice), cards carry the text.
Why this format (from the channel's own numbers): myth-buster shorts at 45–77 s with formulaic "X Didn't Y" titles fell from ~1.3k views to 0–400 and got **zero comments**.
So: shorter, a hook in the first frame, an unsolved question that asks the viewer to vote A/B/C in the comments, staggered publishing (never 4 at once).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Mugshot of a suspect** — one suspect per short. Accuses real people too directly, weak for "what happened" mysteries.
2. **Evidence bag → three exhibits → verdict vote** — opens on one object alone on dark ink, the case file assembles around it, three numbered exhibits, then the viewer is the judge (A/B/C stamps), and the stamp misses: CASE STILL OPEN. *Chosen*: fastest hook, built-in comment prompt, ending loops back to the open file.
3. **Redacted report** — lines unredact one by one. Lovely but slow to read on a phone.

## Logline and arc (per case)
An unsolved historical case in 30 s: what was found → three pieces of evidence → you decide.
Setup: the evidence bag + "CASE No." + headline. Turn: exhibit three (the strangest detail). Ending: three verdict stamps hover, the final stamp misses the page — "CASE OPEN · COMMENT A, B OR C".

## Benchmark
Learn from true-crime short-form explainers (one detail per beat, the question held to the end) and pop-art print design (Ben-Day dots, misregistered headlines). Don't copy any channel's branding, music or layout.

## Shot list (every case)
| # | Beat | Framing / move | Dur | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evidence bag on dark ink: the case's object; UNSOLVED stamp slams; year in giant numerals | locked, flash + shake on stamp | ~4 s | hook in frame 1 |
| 2 | File slides in: case title headline, case no. HUD | folder slide | ~3 s | context |
| 3–5 | Exhibits 1–3: giant halftone numeral, prop drawing, headline, caption bar | dot wipe between, slow push-in | ~5–6 s each | one fact each |
| 6 | VERDICT? A/B/C cards pop | locked frontal, beat pulse | ~5 s | comment prompt |
| 7 | Stamp swings down, misses, lands on the table: CASE OPEN | shake, flash | ~2.5 s | ending + loop |

## Cue map
110 bpm, 4/4. Synth bass 8ths + clap on 2/4 under exhibits; pizzicato + snaps for "secret" beats; silence half a bar before each stamp; a bell arpeggio tag on CASE OPEN.

## Sound design table
| Section | Bed | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | low room tone | stamp boom + click, shutter | silence → hit |
| file | paper | folder slide, typewriter | bass enters |
| exhibits | paper | pop per glyph, whoosh into wipes, a prop sound per exhibit | full groove |
| verdict | — | three pops | bass + snaps |
| end | — | stamp miss thud, crackle | bell tag |

## Type and captions
Headlines: Alfa Slab One (black slab), misregistration shadow. Numerals: Bagel Fat One. HUD: JetBrains Mono 800. Caption bar: dark ink pill, paper text, keyword in highlight ink; carries the spoken line. Palette per case from the style's sets (≤5 inks + paper).
