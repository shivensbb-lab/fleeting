# Facts — Hannibal crosses the Alps
| Claim | Source / basis |
|---|---|
| 218 BC, set out from New Carthage (Cartagena) in Spain against Rome | Polybius 3.33–3.35; Livy 21.21–23 |
| Rome had naval superiority; Hannibal went overland | Standard interpretation (Polybius book 3; e.g. Goldsworthy, *The Fall of Carthage*) |
| ~59,000 men over the Pyrenees (50,000 foot + 9,000 horse) | Polybius 3.35.7 |
| Elephants ferried over the Rhône on rafts | Polybius 3.46 |
| Snow, landslides, attacks by mountain tribes | Polybius 3.50–3.55 |
| The pass is disputed (Clapier, Traversette, Mont Cenis and others proposed) | Ongoing scholarly debate; e.g. Mahaney et al. 2016 (Traversette); de Beer; Lancel. Route drawn schematic |
| The Alpine crossing took 15 days | Polybius 3.56.3 |
| Arrived in Italy with ~26,000 (12,000 African + 8,000 Iberian infantry, ≤6,000 cavalry) | Polybius 3.56.4 |
| Fought in Italy for ~15 years (218–203 BC) | Polybius / Livy; recalled to Africa 203 BC |
1 slab = 5,000 men. Routes schematic.
