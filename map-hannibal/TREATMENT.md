# Hannibal Crosses the Alps — treatment

Style: 3D Map Explainer. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~55 s, English narration (Kokoro bm_lewis).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Funnel** into the Alpine pass. Loses the scale of the march.
2. **Journey along one line** — New Carthage → Ebro → Pyrenees → Rhône → Alps → Po valley; the line ignites leg by leg and the army's pillar shrinks as it goes. *Chosen*: the march is the story; the cost is a quantity.
3. **Two places compared** (Spain vs Italy armies). Lacks motion.

## Logline and arc
In 218 BC Hannibal marched an army from Spain over the Alps into Italy; he got there — with less than half of it.
Setup: a single glowing point at New Carthage on a dark region. Turn: the Alps stand up and the line climbs into them. Ending: the last quantity — 26,000 — held over the Po valley beside the 59,000 that crossed the Pyrenees.

## Benchmark
Learn from documentary aerials of the Alps (raking light, relief) and route-animation explainers (one leg at a time). Don't take their routes as fact: the Alpine pass is disputed — labelled schematic.
