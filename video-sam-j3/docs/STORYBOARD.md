# Storyboard: S.A.M J3 Mall (9:16, 20 s, 60 fps, 1200 frames)

The recurring object is the **bar**: the bar model is Singapore Maths' signature diagram.

## Object chain
1. A crimson bar flies in out of depth and, on "addictive", snaps into 7 blocks (hook).
2. The blocks settle into a bar model on a glass card: 3 crimson + 4 ink blocks, a brace and "7" resolving on "S.A.M" (model).
3. The card's blocks burst into 180 small blocks forming a grid; a counter runs 0 to 180 and gets its "+" on "love" (count).
4. The small blocks stream together into a map pin, which drops onto J3 Mall on a map card of Al Wasl Rd (place).
5. Light dims to a quiet dusk; the pin pulses outward (quiet).
6. A second pin drops: AB Centre, tagged Opening soon; a dashed line draws from J3 Mall to it (ab).
7. The map folds into a crimson Book a free placement button; a pointer taps it on "free" (free).
8. The pressed button blooms into the end card: S.A.M / Seriously Addictive Mathematics / J3 Mall and
   AB Centre (opening soon) / +971 4 375 5154 / @sam.j3malldubai (cta).
