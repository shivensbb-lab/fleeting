# S.A.M — "The S.A.M Way" · treatment

Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`), 9:16, 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~90 s, for Instagram Reels. English narration
(Kokoro `af_heart`, calm and warm), burned-in caption cards and `.srt`. Script and every line: `lines.json`.

Facts used (from the user): S.A.M teaches Singapore Maths; J3 Mall, Al Wasl Rd, 180+ students; new branches in
Meydan and Al Barsha; AB Centre opening soon; Instagram @sam.j3malldubai. No phone, prices or results claims.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Store notice, stretched.** Headline → branches → CTA, one card each. Honest, but it is a 20 s job padded to 90 s,
   and it doesn't show what makes the centre different.
2. **Solve one problem the S.A.M way** (chosen). A real Singapore-Maths word problem, solved live in the method's own
   three steps (Concrete → Pictorial → Abstract) as a classroom how-to: blocks in hands, then a bar model, then the
   number sentence. The payoff turns the bar models into an atomic-age textile; then the proof (180+ students at J3
   Mall), the branches, and a dead-still CTA. The viewer walks away able to do a bar model, which is the best ad a
   maths centre can have.
3. **Do / don't split.** "Guessing" vs "drawing it", rule by rule in two colour blocks. Clear, but it frames other
   ways of learning as wrong and needs claims we can't back.

Chosen: **2**. It is the style's native use (a follow-along how-to, one hand action per step on the beat), it shows the
method instead of saying "we're great", and it ends on the information a parent needs.

## 2. Arc

- **Hook (0–9 s)**: the word problem stamps on, card by card. "Tricky?" — a single held beat.
- **Title (9–13 s)**: "the S.A.M way" in script, S.A.M in slab, sunburst.
- **Step 1 Concrete (13–30 s)**: the child presenter's hands set one block for Ben, three for Ali. Call-out: "same size".
- **Step 2 Pictorial (30–46 s)**: the blocks flatten into bars (iris through medallion 2); bracket "24" lands on the beat.
- **Step 3 Abstract (46–62 s)**: 4 units = 24 → 1 unit = 6 → Ben 6, Ali 18. Stamp ✓.
- **Payoff (62–68 s)**: pull-back: bar models tile into a textile. "Concrete. Pictorial. Abstract."
- **Proof + branches (68–84 s)**: 180+ counter at J3 Mall; colour-block wipe to a map card: J3 Mall, Meydan, Al Barsha
  (new), AB Centre (opening soon) as medallions.
- **CTA (84–90 s)**: @sam.j3malldubai dead still ≥ 3 s in a starburst, iris-out.

## 3. Benchmark

Learn from 1950s classroom films and appliance manuals (one sentence per picture, numbered medallions, round call-outs,
the hand as the step). Don't take any studio's characters, drawings or music. The presenter, the problem and the cards
are ours.

## 5. Score and sound

Original cool-jazz combo at 120 BPM: vibraphone, flute, pizzicato walking bass, brushes, celesta. Each step lands on
its own vibraphone chord (F6, B♭maj7, C9); the branch medallions bring the three back, one each, and a fourth chord for
AB Centre. Double-time feel at the payoff (key up a step); half-time for the proof line.
Silences: A — before "Tricky?" (everything out); B — the beat before "one unit is six" (everything out, then the stamp).
Foley: wooden block clacks, paper swishes on wipes, pen scratches for bars, damped stamps on medallions, rising pips on
the counter, an iris "shhk". Mix: narration on top, music ducks 8 dB, foley unducked, −14 LUFS, TP ≤ −1.2.

## 6. Captions

Paper caption card bottom-centre with small stars, ~46 px Jost (9:16), held ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).
Numbers are digits on screen, words in the voice.
