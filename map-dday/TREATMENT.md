# D-Day: Five Beaches — treatment

Style: 3D Map Explainer. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~50 s, English narration (Kokoro bm_lewis).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Journey** across the Channel. Good motion, but ends without scale.
2. **Flows converge** — routes from southern English ports pour into one assembly area south of the Isle of Wight, then fan out to five beaches; a pillar of men grows over the coast. *Chosen*: the operation's shape *is* convergence.
3. **Before/after** of the coast. No real change of ground to show.

## Logline and arc
On 6 June 1944 nearly 7,000 vessels crossed the Channel to five beaches along 80 km of Normandy; by nightfall about 156,000 men were ashore.
Setup: low over the grey Channel. Turn: the routes converge and fan to the beaches. Ending: the pillar over the coast, then a big pull-out over the whole Channel with every route still glowing (built without a globe to keep the render within budget).

## Benchmark
Learn from news terrain explainers (flows, labels on the ground) and WWII documentary maps (beach sectors). Don't take unit insignia, arrows styles or music.
