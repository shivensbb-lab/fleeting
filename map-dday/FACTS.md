# Facts — D-Day
| Claim | Source / basis |
|---|---|
| 6 June 1944, landings began before dawn (airborne after midnight, seaborne from ~06:30) | US Army Center of Military History; Imperial War Museums |
| Ships sailed from ports along England's south coast (Plymouth, Dartmouth, Portland, Poole, Southampton, Portsmouth, Newhaven among others) | IWM; Royal Navy history of Operation Neptune |
| Assembly "Area Z" south-east of the Isle of Wight, nicknamed "Piccadilly Circus" | IWM / Royal Navy accounts of Operation Neptune |
| Nearly 7,000 vessels | D-Day Center / commonly cited 6,939 vessels (Operation Neptune) |
| Five beaches: Utah, Omaha, Gold, Juno, Sword, along ~50 miles (~80 km) | National WWII Museum; IWM |
| US Rangers climbed the ~30 m (100 ft) cliffs of Pointe du Hoc | US Army CMH, *Small Unit Actions*; NPS/ABMC |
| ~156,000 Allied troops landed by sea and air on D-Day | National WWII Museum; IWM |
Port routes and approach lanes are schematic. 1 slab = 10,000 troops.
