# Napoleon's March on Moscow — treatment

Style: 3D Map Explainer. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, ~55 s, English narration (Kokoro bm_lewis).

## Three candidate structures
1. **Funnel** into Moscow. Misses the return.
2. **Out and back along one line with the quantity riding on it** — the route ignites amber Niemen→Moscow while the army pillar shrinks; the retreat runs back in a second hue; a last pillar of 10,000 stands at the Niemen. *Chosen*: it's the most famous quantity-on-a-map story there is (Minard's 1869 chart), told on real ground.
3. **Two places compared** (Niemen in June vs December). Loses the road between.

## Logline and arc
In June 1812 Napoleon crossed the Niemen with 422,000 men; about 10,000 came back.
Setup: the Niemen at dawn, a tall pillar. Turn: Moscow reached with 100,000, and it is burning/empty — the retreat line ignites back west in winter teal. Ending: the last pillar held over the river beside the ghost of the first.

## Benchmark
Learn from Minard's 1869 flow map (quantity as band width along the route) and from news terrain explainers. Don't copy Minard's drawing; our quantity is pillars on the ground.
