# Facts — Napoleon's Russian campaign, 1812
| Claim | Source / basis |
|---|---|
| June 1812, crossed the Niemen (at Kovno/Kaunas) | Standard chronology (24 June 1812) |
| 422,000 men crossing; ~100,000 reaching Moscow; ~10,000 recrossing the Niemen | Charles Joseph Minard, *Carte figurative…* (1869), the figures used in the chart ("by one famous count"). Modern estimates of totals vary |
| Russian retreat with scorched earth | Standard accounts (e.g. Zamoyski, *1812*) |
| Advance through Vitebsk, Smolensk, Borodino to Moscow (September) | Standard chronology; Moscow entered 14 Sept 1812 |
| Moscow burned; Tsar Alexander I refused to negotiate | Standard accounts |
| Retreat began October; winter, hunger, Cossack attacks; Berezina crossing | Standard accounts; retreat from 19 Oct 1812 |
